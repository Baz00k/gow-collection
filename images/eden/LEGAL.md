# Legal

## What This Image Contains

This image packages the [Eden](https://git.eden-emu.dev/eden-emu/eden) emulator, which is free software licensed under GPL-3.0-only.

It contains **no**:

- Nintendo Switch encryption keys (`prod.keys`, `title.keys`).
- Console firmware.
- Game dumps (`.nsp`, `.xci`, `.nca`, or any other format).

## What You Must Provide

Emulation is legal in many jurisdictions, but the material you run in an emulator is a separate matter.

- **Keys and firmware** must come from a console you own. Do not download them.
- **Games** must come from media you own, dumped yourself, unless the title is legally distributed as homebrew.
